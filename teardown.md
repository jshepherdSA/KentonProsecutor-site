# Teardown — reference sites

Captured via headless-Chrome screenshots, HTML, CSS, and the WordPress REST API.

## kentonprosecutor.org — `[same-org]`
Pull brand, voice, and identity. Do **not** copy structure — except the nav, which
the client explicitly asked to keep similar.

- **Stack:** WordPress, "The Cause" theme (2013), 960px grid, MetaSlider.
- **Header / nav (keep the pattern):**
  - Kenton County Commonwealth's Attorney seal on the left, visually large.
  - Name block: "ROB SANDERS" (large serif caps, navy) over "KENTON COUNTY
    COMMONWEALTH'S ATTORNEY" (serif caps).
  - Full-width slate-blue (#284052) nav bar below, 5 items with dropdowns:
    **About · Services · Resources · News · Contact**. Thin maroon rule above.
  - Dropdowns:
    - About → What We Do, Kenton Commonwealth's Attorney Rob Sanders, Assistant
      Commonwealth's Attorneys (8 bios), Commonwealth's Detective, Victim's Advocate
    - Resources → Law Enforcement Resources, Victim Rights, Victim Resources,
      Student and Job Opportunities (→ Hall of Fame), Links, Subpoenas, FAQs, Videos
- **Brand colors:** navy #004375, slate #284052, deep navy #011b38, maroon #770303 rule.
- **Type:** Georgia/Lora serif headings, sans body.
- **Voice:** first-person letter from Rob; "Seeking Justice Through Community
  Partnerships"; transparency ("keeping Kenton County citizens informed").
- **Assets reused:** office seal, Rob portrait cutout, Beechwood Elementary photo,
  General Assembly testimony, 2023 Redford Award, staff photo, family history photos.
- **Dropped:** image slider, sidebar widgets, Twitter embed, mugshot-led news cards.
